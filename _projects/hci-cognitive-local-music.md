## Overview

This HCI research sequence examined how people discover local artists and how interface design can reduce cognitive friction during exploration. The work combined needfinding, prototyping, and qualitative evaluation.

## Research Focus

- What mental models do users bring to music discovery tasks?
- Which interface choices reduce effort and improve discoverability?
- How can design decisions be justified with user evidence rather than intuition?

## Approach

- Conducted user research and survey-based needfinding
- Built and refined interface concepts through iterative assignments
- Applied HCI principles such as simplicity, consistency, and discoverability
- Evaluated prototypes with qualitative feedback from participants

## Key Findings

- User assumptions often diverged from designer assumptions, reinforcing evidence-first design.
- Discoverability and consistency had outsized impact on perceived usability.
- Iterative, participant-informed design produced stronger outcomes than one-shot mockups.
